02

The First Non-Appearance

Across May and August, Aspire appeared in all 16 of 16 answers tested. On September 9, asked "recommend an orthodontist in Farmington Hills Michigan", Claude returned four practices and Aspire was not among them.

✗
The answer ranked: Jusino → Nakisher → Reynolds → TDR Specialists.

This was not a crawling failure or a site outage. aspire-ortho.com appears twice in that same answer's citation list. The engine read the site, considered the practice, and chose four other names to recommend. A ranking drop means being out-argued; a non-appearance means not making the shortlist at all, and it is the single most important number in this report.

Worth keeping in proportion: on the identical question, Google AI Mode and Perplexity both put Aspire first. This is one platform behaving differently from the other three, not a broad collapse.

03

Why Jusino Overtook Aspire on One Platform

Jusino Orthodontics ranked below Aspire in both the May and August audits. It now leads three of the four Claude answers, including the one Aspire is missing from. The engine states its reasoning directly, which makes this unusually easy to diagnose.

Claude's stated reason, verbatim "On Healthgrades, across 730 reviews, likelihood of recommending him to family and friends sits at about 4.9 out of 5. That's an unusually deep review pool for an orthodontic practice, which makes the rating more trustworthy than a handful of glowing reviews."

That claim checks out at source. Here is the Healthgrades directory for Farmington Hills orthodontists as it stands today:

Healthgrades listing Rating Ratings count
Dr. Thomas Jusino, DDS 4.9 730
Dr. Jason Sherbel, DDS 5.0 2
Dr. Mary Sherbel, DDS 5.0 1
Dr. Andrea Nakisher, DDS 5.0 1
Aspire Orthodontics as a practice listing Does not exist

Aspire has no practice-level Healthgrades page. What exists instead are two personal profiles holding one and two reviews. Healthgrades was cited by all four platforms in this run — eleven times by Claude, four by Perplexity, twice by ChatGPT.

The revealing part: this is a directory artefact, not a real gap On Google, where the review data is verified, the picture reverses entirely. Aspire holds 5.0 across 153 reviews; Jusino holds 4.7 across 119. Aspire has more reviews and a better rating than the practice that is beating it. The 730 figure exists only on Healthgrades, and it is enough to flip one engine's recommendation on its own.
This was quick win #3 in the May audit Claiming the Healthgrades profile was one of three recommendations in the original report, delivered May 22. Three and a half months later it is still unclaimed, and it has moved from a missed opportunity to the measurable cause of a lost recommendation.

The same pattern shows up one directory over. Claude cited Yelp 41 times in this run, more than any other source including Aspire's own website. Aspire had three Yelp reviews at the time of the May audit. The engine that leans hardest on directories is the engine where Aspire lost the most ground, and that is not a coincidence.

04

What Genuinely Improved

Two real wins this cycle, and the first one closes out the headline problem from the August report.

✓
The entity-fragmentation problem is resolved.

In August, ChatGPT resolved the practice as "Dr. Mary Sherbel, DDS, MS Orthodontist" with "Aspire Orthodontics" demoted to a trailing qualifier, and Jason appeared in only one of four answers. That has reversed. All four platforms now lead with the practice name, and ChatGPT names it "Aspire Orthodontics (Drs. Mary & Jason Sherbel)" — brand first, both doctors credited. Jason is named again across the set.

Resolved
✓
Birdeye went from 96 reviews to 162, and the gap to Nakisher halved.

Nakisher sat flat at 242 over the same period, so the gap on that platform closed from 146 to 80. Birdeye is one of the sources ChatGPT leaned on most heavily in August, and it is the most likely driver of the entity fix above. Most recent review posted roughly three weeks ago, so the flow is active.

Improving
Two numbers worth watching Claude still cites Aspire at 96 reviews, the pre-update Birdeye figure. It ranked Aspire #1 on the Invisalign query anyway, so the stale number is not currently costing a position, but it is what the engine is reasoning from. Separately, Google reviews held flat at 153 against Nakisher's 199, so the 46-review gap there is unchanged since May.

09

What's Next

The pattern across three audits is consistent.

Everywhere the AI platforms read Aspire's own website and Google profile, the practice performs well — Google AI Mode and Perplexity both lead with it on three of four queries. Everywhere they read the wider directory layer, it loses to competitors with weaker credentials and deeper profiles. That has been the finding since May, and this cycle it produced the first non-appearance on record.

The directory work is unglamorous and it is not something a website redesign fixes. It is also roughly a quarter's worth of consistent, boring effort, and the measurable upside is the difference between being the third name in an answer and being the first.
