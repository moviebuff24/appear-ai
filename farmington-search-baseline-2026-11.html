01

Completed in November

  • Website move and redirects

    The practice's domain now runs through a Cloudflare account owned by the practice. All 296 addresses from the previous website redirect to the matching page on the new site, so patients and search engines following old links reach the right content.

  • Faster image loading

    Website images are now cached, and the largest photos were compressed. The homepage photo of Dr. Eiwaz went from 531 KB to 58 KB.

  • Email delivery record

    I added a missing email authentication record (SPF), which helps keep practice email out of patients' spam folders.

  • Previous vendor access removed

    As requested, the previous website vendor's administrator access has been removed from Google Analytics. Two other accounts still need your confirmation before I remove them: lseoproduction@localseosites.com, which has full access to Google Search Console, and a pending administrator invitation for lseoproduction@gmail.com in Google Tag Manager.

  • Conversion tracking

    Phone taps and form submissions on the website are now recorded as conversions in Google Analytics. Previously, nothing on the site was counted as a conversion.

  • Google Maps ranking baseline

    I recorded where the practice ranks in Google Maps for the proposed keywords, at 25 locations across Farmington Hills and the surrounding cities (section 05).
