02

Progress Since September

✓
One phone number, and the AI platforms now use it.

In September, two AI answers to the same question gave patients two different numbers. Today both Google profiles, the website, its structured data, Healthgrades and DentistryDirectories all show (248) 516-0004, and Claude, Perplexity and Google AI Mode each gave patients that number, with the correct 12 Mile Rd, Suite 140 address.

✓
Birdeye is claimed and the practice is first on its Farmington Hills list.

In September the profile had never been claimed and sat 19th of 20 on page two. It is now claimed, carries the practice's logo and cover image, and holds the #1 position on page one of Birdeye's Farmington Hills dental directory.

✓
DentistryDirectories is claimed and verified.

The practice entry now carries Verified and Recommended Dentist badges and is listed first for Farmington Hills, under the practice's own name, with the correct address and phone number.

✓
Healthgrades shows the correct office.

The old 32910 W 13 Mile Rd, Suite B200 office is gone from both your profile and the practice entry. Both now show only 32905 W 12 Mile Rd, Suite 140, and your profile shows you as accepting new patients.

✓
New listings on Foursquare, the Better Business Bureau and Nextdoor.

Foursquare matters beyond its own audience because it supplies location data to many other apps and map services, which is how an old address keeps reappearing on sites nobody updated.

✓
The website now lists the insurance it accepts.

The "We Accept the Following Insurance" section, empty in October, now names ten carriers, including Delta Dental, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian and GEHA. Insurance is the first filter most patients apply, and Delta Dental's own directory is the source behind the practice's new-patient mentions on Perplexity and Claude.

✓
Google AI Mode now describes the practice the way you want it described.

Its reasons for recommending the practice today are evening hours until 7 PM on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, same-day emergency evaluations, and clear communication. It also named the practice for implants for the first time, as "all-in-one general dental care paired with implant restoration."

03

What Has Not Moved Yet

The two broad questions, "best dentist" and "best cosmetic dentist", have not improved. The practice is named on one of those eight answers, by Google AI Mode at #4. Google AI Mode also no longer names the practice for cosmetic dentistry, and it moved the practice from first to sixth on the new-patient question, below Superior Family Dental, Barone Family Dental and Gentle Touch Family Dental Care.

Two things explain most of this. First, AI platforms re-read directories on their own schedule, and a correction made in October can take several weeks or more to appear in an answer. Second, and more important, ChatGPT is no longer reading the directories it relied on in September. The two listings corrected first, Birdeye and DentistryDirectories, were the right targets based on the September data. ChatGPT has since moved most of its attention to other sites, which is the subject of the next section.

What's Next

The listings corrected so far are right. The next step is the sites ChatGPT reads now.

Every listing corrected since September shows the right address and phone number, and the platforms that rely on those listings are giving patients the right details. The broader questions will take longer. ChatGPT in particular has moved to a new set of directories, and the most-cited of them shows the practice at its old office with no credit for its reviews.

My plan for this month is to claim and correct DentistsRanked, remove the last of the old phone numbers, complete the pending verifications, and re-run this audit in early December to measure the effect.
